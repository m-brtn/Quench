# Frame-time benchmark of the hello example (docs/performance.md), at a fixed frame size and the default vsync:
#   hello --frame-size 1920x1080 --script examples/hello/scenarios/benchmark.txt
# The perf line in the log has the frame, CPU and GPU times. The perf region runs in real time like normal play
# (10 s: about 600 frames at 60 Hz, 1200 at 120 Hz). GPU time is the cost to compare: frame time follows the display,
# and on macOS a window is paced to the display even with --present-mode immediate, which also inflates the measured
# GPU time (docs/performance.md).

wait 2
perf_begin hello
wait 10
perf_end
expect perf_frames >= 300
quit
