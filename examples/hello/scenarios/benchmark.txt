# Frame-time benchmark of the hello example (docs/performance.md), at a fixed frame size and the default vsync:
#   hello --frame-size 1920x1080 --script examples/hello/scenarios/benchmark.txt
# The perf line in the log has the frame, CPU and GPU times; app time advances 1/120 s per frame, so the region is
# 1200 frames. GPU time is the cost to compare: frame time follows the display, and on macOS a window is paced to the
# display even with --present-mode immediate, which also inflates the measured GPU time (docs/performance.md).

wait 2
perf_begin hello
wait 10
perf_end
expect perf_frames >= 1199
quit
