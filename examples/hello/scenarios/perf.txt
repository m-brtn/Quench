# Perf region check of the hello example (ctest: hello_perf_<backend>, a part of check.txt): an offscreen region in
# real time, where the GPU time of every frame is read once, when its fence has signalled, and counted in the region
# its frame was in. No timed frame is lost or counted twice (perf_gpu_lost: the region's frames on a device that
# times them, less the samples and the frames still in flight at the end; 0 without GPU timing, Vulkan through
# MoltenVK).

wait 0.1
perf_begin hello_perf
wait 0.25
perf_end
expect perf_frames > 10
expect perf_gpu_lost == 0
perf_begin hello_perf_2
wait 0.25
perf_end
expect perf_frames > 10
expect perf_gpu_lost == 0
