# An HDR frame in the capture (ctest: demo_hdr_on_metal, demo_hdr_on_vulkan, run with --hdr on --hdr-headroom 4): the
# output is HDR extended linear with headroom 4 whatever the display reports. The capture at 2 s is the frame clipped
# at paper white (golden demo_hdr_on); its brightest value, before clipping, is above paper white and at most the
# headroom.

set dither 0
wait 0.2
expect hdr == 1
expect hdr_headroom == 4
until capture_peak > 0 3
expect capture_peak > 2
expect capture_peak <= 4.01
quit
