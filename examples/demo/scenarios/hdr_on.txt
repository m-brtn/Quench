# An HDR frame in the capture (ctest: demo_hdr_on_<backend>, a part of batch.txt, which runs with --hdr-headroom 4):
# the HDR section from its start state with the output switched to HDR extended linear, headroom 4 whatever the
# display reports. The capture at 2 s is the frame clipped at paper white (golden demo_hdr_on); its brightest value,
# before clipping, is above paper white and at most the headroom. The output goes back to SDR at the end.
section hdr
reset_section
reset_camera
hdr on
set dither 0
wait 0.2
expect hdr == 1
expect hdr_headroom == 4
until time >= 2 3
windows off
capture demo_hdr_on.png
windows on
expect capture_peak > 2
expect capture_peak <= 4.01
hdr off
until hdr == 0 1
quit
