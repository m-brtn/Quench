# Section switching (ctest: demo_cycle_<backend>, a part of batch_metal.txt and batch_native.txt): three rounds through
# every section, with a reset of each in the first and the third. The first round creates or resets every section (and
# waits for the assets of those that load some), so that the parts before it in a batch leave no settings behind; after
# it, switching leaves the scene and the log as they are, the GPU resources within 3 (fences and transfer buffers of the
# frames and uploads in flight come and go), and a reset makes the same section again (the assets section logs its
# broken and missing model again, on purpose). The memory is marked after the second round and grows by less than
# 64 MB in the third (allocator noise; a section that leaked its scene at every switch would be more): after the
# first round alone, the footprint swung between -24 and +47 MB on the native backend, where the ray tracing section's
# filter histories (about 25 MB by their formats at this size) come and go and the driver hands freed GPU memory back
# late; over 20 rounds of resets it moved within about 60 MB from round to round with the tunnel's lamps and drive
# moving and with them still, without a trend, the GPU resources the same in every round.

wait 0.1
section materials
reset_section
wait 0.03

reset_section
