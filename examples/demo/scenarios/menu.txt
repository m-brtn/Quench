# Menu navigation of the engine demo (ctest: demo_menu_<backend>, a part of batch.txt): keyboard, gamepad, mouse and
# touch switch sections, H hides the windows. Any window size: the click and the touch go to the menu entries the demo
# names (`@menu:<section>`).
section materials

wait 0.1
expect section == 0
expect sections == 11
tap ]
until section == 1 1
tap PageDown
until section == 2 1
tap [
until section == 1 1
tap PageUp
until section == 0 1
tap 5
until section == 4 1
# The tenth section is on key 0.
tap 0
until section == 9 1
tap 5
until section == 4 1

gamepad attach
until gamepad == 1 2
gamepad button rightshoulder tap
until section == 5 1
gamepad button rightshoulder tap
until section == 6 1
gamepad button rightshoulder tap
until section == 7 1
gamepad button rightshoulder tap
until section == 8 1
gamepad button rightshoulder tap
until section == 9 1
# The eleventh section has no key; the shoulders reach it.
gamepad button rightshoulder tap
until section == 10 1
gamepad button rightshoulder tap
until section == 0 1
gamepad button leftshoulder tap
until section == 10 1
gamepad detach
until gamepad == 0 2

tap H
until ui == 0 1
tap H
until ui == 1 1

# Mouse and touch on the list: entry 2 (Shadows), entry 4 (Post-processing) and entry 11 (Ray tracing).
click @menu:shadows
until section == 1 1

until section == 3 1

until section == 10 1

# Keyboard and gamepad on the windows: Tab or the D-pad give them the keys and buttons (the scene's actions stop),
# Down and Enter (the D-pad and A) choose an entry, Escape (B) gives the keys back to the scene.
section materials

tap Down
wait 0.05
tap Return

tap Escape

wait 0.05
gamepad button dpdown tap
wait 0.05
