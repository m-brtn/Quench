# Physics in the wind (ctest: demo_physics_wind_<backend>, a part of batch.txt and batch_native.txt; no capture):
# quench::WindDrag pushes the Physics section's bodies by their drag in the weather's wind field. Light balls put down
# upwind roll downwind (+x) in a gale of 20 m/s at 10 m, about 5 m/s at their height; without wind they stay.
section physics
reset_section

wait 0.1
expect bodies == 21
set light_balls 3
wait 1.5
expect light_balls == 3
expect light_ball_x < -3.4
expect wind_at_floor == 0
set wind 20
wait 0.1
expect wind == 20
expect wind_at_floor > 3
expect wind_at_floor < 7
wait 3
expect light_ball_x > -3
set wind 0
quit
