# The ray-traced indirect light in motion and after a sudden change (docs/performance.md, "The denoiser in a still
# scene"): the ray tracing section's phone defaults with per-pixel indirect light, converged still for 120 frames;
# then for 120 frames (2 s at the phone's 60 Hz) the camera pans and the sun turns as they would on the phone (0.5
# and 0.33 degrees every 2 frames: 15 and 10 degrees per second at 60 Hz), the character walks and the torus spins
# (at app time: half their speed per frame on the phone, since an offscreen frame is 1/120 s), captured at 30, 60
# and 120; then everything stops at once and the frame of the same instant settles for 600 frames: the difference
# of the two is the noise and the lag in motion. Then, from the start camera and still again, the sun jumps by 60
# degrees; captures 1 to 64 frames after it and the settled frame 600 frames after it. Offscreen on the native
# backend, the captures into the user directory:
#   quench-demo --gpu-backend metal --frame-size 1920x1080 --audio-device null --user-dir DIR \
#     --script raytracing_noise_motion.txt

set denoiser temporal
set indirect rt
set upscaling spatial
set scale 0.5

camera 18 26 10.5
mark frame
until d_frame >= 120 10
