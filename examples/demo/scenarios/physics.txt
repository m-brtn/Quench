# Physics (ctest: demo_physics_metal, demo_physics_vulkan): dropping bodies by key and gamepad, reset, blasts by key
# and by a click on the floor. The capture is taken at 2 s, with the blasted bodies in the air. Any window size: the
# click goes to the section's named point `floor`, with the windows hidden (they cover the floor on a portrait phone).

set dither 0
wait 0.1
expect bodies == 21
tap B
until bodies == 22 1
tap N
until bodies == 23 1
gamepad attach
until gamepad == 1 2
gamepad button x tap
until bodies == 24 1
gamepad button y tap
until bodies == 25 1
gamepad detach
until gamepad == 0 2
tap R
until bodies == 21 1
# The reset stack stands: its top box (row 5 of 0.7 m boxes) is at about 3.15 m.
wait 0.3
expect highest > 3
expect fastest < 0.5

# Blasts: P at the center, then a click on the floor in front of the stack.
tap P
until blasts == 1 1
wait 0.05
expect fastest > 3
tap H
until ui == 0 1
click @floor
until blasts == 2 1
expect clicks == 1
tap H
until ui == 1 1
wait 0.3
expect highest > 1
until time >= 2.1 3
quit
