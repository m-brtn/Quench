- **13:56-14:35 — View rays far from the origin** (a user report from Cloudsong on the Mac: the distant ground, and
  the clouds when on, trembled up and down while the character stood still; game: `../cloudsong/devlog/2026-11-08.md`).

  clouds below the camera, coherently up and down, most on one TAA jitter sample of eight. Cause:
  `quenchViewRay`, the clouds' `cloudViewRay` and the rain shafts' rays took a point on the near plane (0.1 m away)

  offset of about e (1 - a) / a. Fix: the rays are read at depth 0 of the renderer's infinite reversed projection,
  `normalize(mul(invViewProj, float4(ndc, 0, 1)).xyz)`, where the inverse holds no translation (the path tracer
  already read its rays 100 km out and is unchanged). New test `renderer_view_rays_far_from_the_origin`; the demo's

## Problems and solutions

- **Splashes under the ground.** Cause: the rain occlusion map was drawn by the shadow cascades' pipelines, whose
  slope-scaled depth bias pushed the map's depth about 0.37 m down where the rain falls slanted, so the splashes,
  placed on the map's highest surface, were under the paving. Solution: unbiased occluder pipelines for the map.
- **Snow's shafts three times the rain's cost.** Cause: a cbuffer struct passed by value to a function called per
  step was copied whole every step. Solution: read the cbuffer in place (`shaftSnow`), with a comment saying why.
- **Snow under the shelter's upwind half.** Cause: the occlusion map along the flakes' near-horizontal drift.
  Solution: its direction clamped to 0.3 horizontal over vertical under snow; the snow presets' wind 2 m/s.
- **The view over the shelter under the ground.** Cause: the demo's pitch is positive looking down. Solution: +38.
- **`rain_cost.txt` measured the snow too.** Cause: the weather section's default snow (Medium) is on in every
  preset. Solution: `set snow off` in the scenario.
- **The meadow cost 0.2 ms of CPU per frame.** Cause: one instance per tuft of grass (2131 instances), each
  culled, sorted and uploaded every frame. Solution: 64 instances of grass patches with the sway rooted per vertex
  (`quenchWindSwayAbout`); the frame's CPU median back to 0.95 ms (0.97 without the meadow).
- **The gusts doubled the drops' cost.** Cause: three value noises per drop for the eddies. Solution: one hash per
  lattice corner split into three 10-bit values (`quenchWindNoise3`): 0.611 to 0.443 ms with the image unchanged
  in kind (the wind's captures were made after it).
- **Snow drawn as rain with the weather's clock stopped.** Cause: the wetness grid's precipitation and snow share,
  what the drops and flakes read, were sampled only when the clock moved, so a scenario that changed the settings
  at time scale 0 drew the previous fall. Solution: the settings and the cells mark the grid stale and the next
  `update()` samples it again, also one of 0 s. The rain's three captures changed by it (with the veil fix below:
  numbers in Results).
- **`audio_clipped` in the windy storm.** Cause: heavy rain, a gale and near thunder together went over full scale.
  Solution: the heavy rain's default loop from RMS 0.2 to 0.13 (the thunder's and the wind's defaults at 0.10 and
  0.11); the storm part with lightning at rate 24 now plays 3 or more thunders without clipping.
- **The native backend's wind captures differed from SDL GPU's.** Cause: waits of whole seconds ended a frame early
  or late by the rounding of the app's clock, moving the streaks by a frame. Solution: waits that end between two
  frames (1.504 s, 0.054 s), as the animation scenarios do; the native backend's captures are the same images now.
- **The storm's shafts brighter than its clouds** (part 2's grey veil). Cause and solution in Stages (02:40).
- **Lightning:** the first rule flashed far too often under a cumulonimbus field and the first flash was too weak
  to see; strikes are now kept by both the type (0.75 to 0.95) and the precipitation (0.4 to 0.8) at their place,
  and the scene's light halves at 2 km, the clouds' glow at 6 km. The plants were too stiff to see in the first
  captures; the bend is now about 0.08 radians per m/s at stiffness 1. The first light balls in the physics section
  flew over its rails in a gale; they are 1.5 kg hollow balls now.
- **`weather_wind_offset_and_period` failed under Node.js** (the offset's rate 0.0428 m/s off the gust, the limit
  2 % of 1.81 m/s; 0.0168 on the Mac). Cause: the test began at 200 s, where the air has travelled 3 km; the field
  subtracts that distance (a float, rounded to about 0.1 mm) from the point, and over a 0.01 s step the rounding
  alone is a few hundredths of a m/s of the rate; the Mac's fused multiply-adds round less than WebAssembly's
  separate operations. Not an error of the field. Solution: the test starts at 20 s (300 m of air): 0.0012 m/s on
  the Mac, 0.0013 under Node.js; `docs/weather.md` says how the air's distance rounds over time (4 mm after an
  hour at 20 m/s).

## Limitations and open questions

- Nobody has looked at the snow in motion or on a phone; check mode item `weather.snow` asks for it. Not run on an
  iPhone; the phone defaults' cost (about 1 ms on the iPhone 17) is an estimate by the stage 11 ratio. (03:41: the
  user looked at the weather on the Mac and the iPhone 17 and reported it fine, see Stages; the cost on the phone
  is still not measured.)
- The snow cover is a whitening of open ground, not geometry (no depth, drifts or footprints); beyond 64 m floors
  under roofs whiten too; the grid melts by the temperature at y = 0, and the estimate after `setTime` assumes the
  present temperature held. Sleet is a mix of the rain's and the snow's drawing.
- In the snow showers' capture a sunbeam through a gap in the clouds shows as a bright line in the shafts, and a few
  flakes near the camera are dark specks against the bright plain (accepted).
- The storm from the air as a grey veil (part 2's polish item) is not addressed yet. (Addressed at 02:40, in Stages: the veil darker, as dark as
  the clouds' bases; from inside the rain it stays an even veil of about 1 km visibility, no curtains at that range.)
- Nobody has looked at the wind in motion (the plants swaying, gusts sweeping through the rain, the lightning
  flickering) or listened to the wind and the thunder; check mode items `weather.wind` and `weather.wind_sound` ask
  for it. Not run on an iPhone; the phone defaults' cost there an estimate. (03:41: the user looked at the wind and
  listened to it and the thunder on the Mac and the iPhone 17, "yes, everything is fine", see Stages; the cost on
  the phone is still an estimate.)
- The wind knows no geometry (no slowdown behind hills or buildings); the sway is a rigid turn about a root with a
  height falloff, not a simulated stem; motion vectors, the acceleration structures and rays see the plants unbent
  (TAA reprojects them as if still); `WindDrag` has no torque, lift or shelter; the clouds drift with the mean wind
  only; no bolts are drawn and the sky cube does not flash.
- `dist/` holds no packages since 03:27:55 (the 0.9.0 and 0.10.0 ones the task kept included); the iPhone 17 has
  the `b91aff0` demo installed but not launched. Since 03:34 it holds only `QuenchDemo-0.10.0-645405e-*` (macOS zip
  and iOS zip with the `.ipa`); the user removed the others. (Since 04:08 only the 0.11.0 packages, see Results.)
- Release 0.11.0 ran nowhere on a phone: the iPhone 17 has it installed but not launched (its device tests last ran
  on 2026-11-07 with the native default's code), the iPhone 18 Pro Max ran nothing of it. The release commit
  changed docs and the version only after the last full gate, so no benchmark on the screen was run for it.
- The cloud shadow map has one plane (`cloudShadowHeight`): ground far above or below it still reads the column its
  sun ray crosses at that height. Not run on a phone.
- The view ray fix was measured offscreen on the Mac only; whether the shaking is gone on the screen is for the user
  to see. Other places that subtract the camera's position from a nearby world position (surfaces' view vectors,
  `RayCommon.hlsli`'s ray origins) keep float precision relative to their distance (0.24 mm at 2.2 km, below a
  pixel at a metre); a world of tens of kilometres would still want camera-relative rendering, which the engine does
  not have.
- Particles and trails ran nowhere on a phone, on the screen or in motion before a person (check mode item
  `particles.motion` asks for it); the costs are offscreen on the Mac only. Not in the ray tracing structures or
  the path tracer; no sorting within a system (systems and trails are sorted by one distance each); every slot of a
  drawn system is stepped and drawn (no compaction or indirect draws); no collisions; trails are built on the CPU;
  the particles' clock wraps every 4096 s. DXIL of the particle shaders is compiled only on Windows hosts, like
  every engine shader (`docs/particles.md`, "Limitations").
- The 14:39 report's fixes were looked at only in captures offscreen on the Mac: nobody has looked at the new rain,
  clouds and shafts in motion on the screen or on a phone; no costs on a phone. The shafts cost up to 11 % more
  from the ground (the snow's 10 to 15 %, mostly the smoothing pass), the clouds 2 to 4 %.
- Left in the weather after the fixes: a snow shower's shaft seen from afar reads as a band with a flat top (the
  shower's cell under the cloud base); in a dark storm the streaks are hard to see (as in real rain, but the demo
  may want them stronger); the demo's ground is finite, so the shafts' rays past it end on a plane at y = 0; the
  splashes are one procedural crown, repeated. The drops follow the gusts as the air does, without inertia.
- One Vulkan validation error through MoltenVK in one of three batch runs (Problems), not explained.
