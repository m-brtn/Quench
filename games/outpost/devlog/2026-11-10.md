## Problems and solutions

- **Everything black in the first capture.** Blender's glTF exporter writes materials without backface culling as
  double-sided, so the outline shells were drawn from the front. `use_backface_culling = True` on the outline
  material.
- **The autopilot stood still next to the tanks.** A cell had been placed where the walker could not get to.
  `spawnCell` now keeps only points with a complete path from the pad in front of the door
  (`NavQuery::findPath`, not `partial`), and a walker that stands still for 1.5 s on its way to a cell has that cell
  placed again.
- **The navmesh let the walker's body into the props.** The engine marks `Geometry::boxes` after the walkable area
  is eroded by the agent's radius (`src/navigation/Build.cpp`), so a not-walkable box keeps no distance by itself.
  The game grows its blockers by 0.85 of the walker's radius. Observed, nothing changed in the engine.
- **The pods could not be seen behind the door's lintel** from the camera's 33 degrees. Moved forward to 1.9 m
  behind the door line, the bay's lights in front of them.
- **`click @door` did nothing in a 640x360 window** (the template's ctest size): the panel covered the door. The
  test runs at 1280x720.
- **A sun 3.5 degrees up gave a flat orange picture.** The second time of day is the blue hour, the sun 7 degrees
  below the horizon, where the lamps light the pad.
- **A model darkened in bands when the camera is close, with the outlines on.** Cause: its outline hull, 4.5 to
  7 cm around it, was a shadow caster; close up the shadow maps resolve that gap. Solution: the hulls cast no
  shadows.
- **The click marker half hidden.** It was one flat ring at the height of the ground under its center: across the
  pad's edge (20 cm), on the ramp and over the ramp's lip, teeth and curbs the part over higher ground was below
  it. Solution: the ring follows the ground point by point.

## Limitations and open questions

- Nobody has played it: no run in a window, so the mouse (wheel, right drag, real clicks), touch and the feel of
  the walk in motion are unverified. Scripted clicks and keys only.
- Not run on SDL GPU, Vulkan through MoltenVK, iOS or Windows. On the web only in headless Chrome on the Mac; the
  user tried the local page before asking for the deployment and reported nothing about it. No frame times
  measured.
- The walker's legs are procedural (a cycle by distance walked, IK); its feet slide when it turns in place. No
  sound. The outlines are an inverted hull: thin details have none, and they are off with ray tracing.
- The models follow a concept picture whose source and licence are unknown (`assets_src/models/CREDITS.md`).
- `games/` is not in the engine's `.gitignore`: the directory shows as untracked there. Nothing is committed.
- The ripple's fix was looked at in offscreen captures only; the web page still has the engine before it.
- The marker lies on the ground and on what the walker steps over, not on objects: a barrel or a crate standing
  on the ring still covers that part of it, as it covers the ground. `tools/make_models.py` does not write the
  same bytes twice (two runs: `base.glb` 1953536 bytes with different hashes; the committed one 1958040; 720
  objects and 81516 triangles each time).
