# Credits

Every `.glb` file in this directory is written by `tools/make_models.py` (Blender, headless): geometry from the
script's own primitives, flat-color materials, no textures. The digits ("10", "320") are meshes of Blender's built-in
font (Bfont, a derivative of Bitstream Vera Sans; its licence allows use and redistribution).

The layout and the look of the outpost and of the walker follow a concept picture supplied by the author of this
experiment (an isometric outpost with a hangar, a tower marked "10", fuel tanks, lamps and a spherical walker). The
models were built from scratch after it, nothing was copied or traced from the file, and the picture itself is not in
this repository. Its source and licence are not recorded here: clear them before publishing these models or anything
made with them.
