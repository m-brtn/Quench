# Smoke check (ctest `smoke`): the models load, the navmesh is built, a click sends the walker to a cell and to the
# door, the cell is delivered, and the autopilot brings the first pod online. App time advances 1/120 s per frame in
# scripted runs.
# The window is 1280x720 points in ctest: in a smaller one the panel covers the door the script clicks.
#   outpost --window 1280x720 --script scenarios/smoke.txt

until models_ready == 1 10
until nav_ready == 1 10
until cells == 5 5
expect carried == 0
expect ray_shadows == 0

# A click on the pad: the walker walks there.
mark walker_x
click @pad
until d_walker_x < -6 8
expect walker_speed > 1

# The keys steer it, relative to the camera: W is away from the camera (-x and -z at the start's azimuth).
until walker_speed < 0.2 6
mark walker_z
down W
wait 0.8
up W
expect d_walker_z < -1

# A click on the nearest cell picks it up; a click at the door delivers it.
click @cell
until carried == 1 20
click @door
until delivered == 1 20
expect carried == 0
expect walker_y > 0.4

# The autopilot gathers by itself: the first pod comes online after three cells.
autopilot on
until pods_online >= 1 60
expect delivered >= 3

# The blue hour: the sun is down and the lamps take over.
sun -7
wait 0.2
expect night > 0.9
capture smoke.png
quit
