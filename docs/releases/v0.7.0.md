# Quench 0.7.0

2026-11-06. Stage 9 of the [roadmap](../roadmap.md): a native Metal backend with GPU timestamps per pass, hardware
ray tracing (sun shadows and ambient occlusion) and MetalFX upscaling. Source only. Changes since
[0.6.0](v0.6.0.md) (tag `v0.6.0`). Everything new here is opt-in and ran on the Mac only (the native backend also
in the iOS Simulator, without ray tracing or MetalFX): the default stays SDL GPU and shadow maps until they have
run on an iPhone (see "What was not verified").

## What changed

- **Native Metal backend of `quench::gpu`** ([Architecture](../architecture.md#native-metal-backend),
  [Platforms](../platforms.md#native-metal-backend)), opt-in on Apple platforms with `--gpu-backend metal`,
  `AppDesc::gpuBackend`, `gpu::DeviceDesc::backend` or `QUENCH_GPU_BACKEND=metal`; the default stays SDL GPU. It
  covers everything the SDL GPU backend does (resources, render, compute and copy passes, uploads, readbacks, a
  `CAMetalLayer` swapchain with vsync and the four compositions: SDR, SDR linear, extended linear (EDR) and HDR10;
  ImGui, resizing) with the same metallibs and binding slots. Documented differences: buffer and texture cycling is
  a no-op (Metal's hazard tracking serializes), blits from 2D and 2D array textures only, no mailbox present mode,
  HDR10 on macOS only.
- **GPU timestamps per pass** (`Caps::gpuPassTiming`, the native backend only). `CommandBuffer::beginTiming()` /
  `endTiming()` mark ranges; the app times `game`, `ui` and `blit`, the renderer its passes (upload, shadows, hdr,
  taa, bloom, tonemap, and the ray tracing and MetalFX passes). The times are busy time, not start to end, since
  passes overlap on Apple GPUs. Off unless something reads them: `Device::setPassTiming()`,
  `App::requestPassTiming()`, `--gpu-pass-timing` or a scenario's perf region (`perf_pass_*` values).
- **DXC for ray query shaders.** Entries marked `:rayquery` compile through DXC v1.9.2609, built from its pinned
  source archives once per machine into `~/Library/Caches/Quench` (macOS has no DXC release binary); glslang keeps
  compiling every other entry, so their outputs are unchanged.
- **Acceleration structures** in `quench::gpu` on the native backend (`Caps::rayTracing`;
  [Architecture](../architecture.md#ray-tracing)): a bottom level per mesh, a top level rebuilt every frame from the
  scene's instances, skinned instances refitted every frame and built again after 60 refits (staggered between
  instances); render and compute passes bind a top level for ray queries in their shaders.
- **Ray-traced sun shadows and ambient occlusion** in `quench_render`
  ([Render module](../render.md#ray-traced-shadows-and-ambient-occlusion)), as slots with fallbacks by
  `gpu::Caps`: shadows by rays (fallback: the cascaded shadow maps) with cut-out holes, tinted translucent casters
  and posed skinned casters, soft from the sun's cone with a depth-aware blur and TAA's history (1 or 2 rays per
  pixel); ambient occlusion by rays (fallback: none). The defaults stay shadow maps and no occlusion.
- **MetalFX upscaling** ([Upscaling](../render.md#upscaling)), spatial and temporal (`Caps::spatialUpscaling`,
  `temporalUpscaling`; not in the iOS Simulator's SDK); temporal replaces TAA's resolve. Fallback: bilinear scaling
  with TAA or FXAA, the default.
- **Engine demo** ([Engine demo](../demo.md)). An eleventh section, "Ray tracing" (no number key; `[`, `]`, the
  page keys or the list): soft ray-traced shadows through a fence and a tinted glass, a walking character's posed
  shadow, contact occlusion, MetalFX at render scales down to 0.5, and its fallbacks on SDL GPU. Its checks run on
  the native backend and on the fallbacks (Metal through SDL GPU, Vulkan through MoltenVK), each with its golden
  image; `scenarios/raytracing_benchmark.txt` measures the options one by one. The other sections' goldens changed
  only by the menu's eleventh line. ctest also renders hello and two demo sections on the native backend against the
  `metal` goldens.
- **Fixed:** SDL GPU's mipmap generation on Vulkan for textures that are not square (SDL 3.4.18 does not stop the
  last levels at 1 pixel, which the validation layer reported): the backend now blits the levels itself there.

## What was verified

From the [devlog of 2026-11-06](../../devlog/2026-11-06.md). Mac (Apple M4 Pro) and the iOS Simulator only.

- At the release commit: format check clean; `debug` and `release` build without warnings, ctest 377 tests each,
  359 passed, 18 skipped (the ray tracing cases on SDL GPU and on Vulkan through MoltenVK, which have no ray
  tracing, and the two audio device cases), among them the native backend's cases under the Metal API validation
  layer and the demo's checks on Metal, on Vulkan through MoltenVK with the validation layers and on the native
  backend; `tsan` 377 tests with the same 18 skipped, without a ThreadSanitizer report; `wasm-node` under Node.js
  113 tests, 106 passed, 7 skipped (the cases that need threads); in the iOS Simulator (iPhone 18 Pro Max,
  iOS 27.0) `ios-sim-tests` 263 tests, 241 passed, 22 skipped (ray tracing and MetalFX, which the Simulator lacks,
  and the window, headroom and opt-in device output cases); `tools/new_game.py`: the generated game builds against
  this checkout, starts and quits; `--version` prints 0.7.0; captures looked at.
- On the code of this release before the release commit (which adds only the version and documentation): no
  warnings in the `asan`, `host-tools`, `ios-sim-release`, `ios-device-release` and `wasm-node` builds; `asan`
  ctest 377 tests with the same 18 skipped, without an AddressSanitizer or UndefinedBehaviorSanitizer report; the
  generated game also starts on the native backend.
- Research before the ray tracing code: MoltenVK (v1.4.2 and `main`) exposes no Vulkan ray tracing extension, so
  Vulkan through MoltenVK always takes the fallbacks; glslang's HLSL front end cannot compile ray queries; DXC's
  SPIR-V through SPIRV-Cross to MSL 3.0 runs (a test kernel's ray hit a triangle at the expected distance 1.0).
- Native backend parity, Release: hello and all eleven demo captures of the first half (ten sections and HDR on)
  identical to the Metal golden images (mean difference 0.0000, largest pixel difference 0); the Audio section's
  WAV byte-identical to SDL GPU's; 25 `gpu_metal_native.*` and 26 `render_metal_native.*` cases pass under
  `MTL_DEBUG_LAYER=1`, among them the window, present and all four swapchain compositions. GPU timestamps: period
  1.0000 ns on the M4 Pro; the top-level ranges cover 99.5 to 100 % of the frame's GPU time.
- Ray tracing, measured: ray-traced against mapped hard shadows, 317 shadowed and 598 lit pixels in both; a moved
  caster moves the shadow without building a bottom level again; a translucent glass at opacity 0.5 lets through
  0.504 of grey light (maps 0.497) and 0.504 / 0.280 / 0.280 of red; a skinned caster's first frame builds 2 bottom
  levels, the next frames refit 1, and the shadow shows the posed hole; occlusion 0.822 of the open light next to a
  box, 1.000 away from it. MetalFX against native TAA: spatial from scale 0.5 mean 1.63, temporal mean 1.15.
- Performance, Release with vsync at about 120 Hz, 1920 x 1080, medians in ms ([Performance](../performance.md)).
  SDL GPU before and after the second half: hello CPU p50 +0.4 %, p99 -6.2 %; every demo section within 5 %. The
  native backend without timestamps is not slower on the CPU than SDL GPU (materials CPU p50 1.033 against 1.094,
  shadows 0.898 against 0.948, animation 1.544 against 1.554); timestamps cost 0.09 to 0.14 ms of CPU. The Ray
  tracing section on the native backend, CPU p50 / GPU p50: ray-traced shadows and occlusion 1.420 / 6.184, shadows
  only 1.478 / 5.961, hard shadows (1 ray, no blur) 1.416 / 5.551, shadow maps 1.287 / 4.906: about 1 ms of GPU time
  more for rays. MetalFX temporal at scale 1 / 0.5: GPU 6.513 / 5.593 ms (MetalFX itself about 2.1 ms); spatial at
  0.5: 4.604. Skinned bottom levels for 259 characters: 1.57 ms with refits against 5.12 ms building every frame.

## What was not verified

- Nothing of 0.7.0 has run on an iPhone: not the native backend, EDR through its own `CAMetalLayer`, 120 Hz,
  timestamps on the A20 Pro, `Caps::rayTracing` or MetalFX on the device, nor the ray tracing benchmark at 120 Hz
  that is to choose the default per device. The iOS Simulator has neither ray tracing nor MetalFX.
- Nobody has watched the Ray tracing section in motion (noise and ghosting with 1 and 2 shadow rays and with MetalFX
  temporal); it was checked by numbers and still captures. MetalFX temporal's jitter and motion conventions were
  found by measurement against TAA in one scene, not from documentation.
- ctest repeats only three native capture checks (hello, the Animation section, HDR on) and the Ray tracing
  section; the other demo sections were compared on the native backend once by hand.
- The native backend's swapchain compositions were created and drawn by the test cases on this Mac, whose display
  has no HDR range: EDR and HDR10 output were not seen on an HDR display. Ray tracing on Vulkan never ran (MoltenVK
  has none); DXIL of the ray query entries is not built on the Mac.

## Changes for games

What a game on 0.6.0 changes or checks when it moves to 0.7.0. Nothing was renamed or removed.

- **Surface shaders: fragment texture and sampler slot 6 are taken** by the ray mask (`quenchRayMask` in
  `quench/Surface.hlsli`), and `FrameUniforms::options.w` carries its bits; `quenchTransmittanceOf` is the shared
  transmittance function. A custom surface that used slot 6 moves to another one.
- **GPU pass timing is off by default.** `Device::setPassTiming(true)` turns it on, `App::requestPassTiming()` for
  the frames a game shows the ranges, `--gpu-pass-timing` for every frame, and a scenario's perf region; otherwise
  `app::FrameStats::gpuPasses` is empty (and it fills a few frames after timing is turned on).
- **`--gpu-backend` and `--gpu-pass-timing` are engine flags now** (with the environment variable
  `QUENCH_GPU_BACKEND`): a game flag of the same name no longer reaches the game.
- **`PassContext::depth` is empty in `PostHdr` passes** while MetalFX upscaling runs (the image is at the target
  size then).
- **`ShaderResources` has one more member**, `accelerationStructures`: tables generated by `quench_add_shaders`
  include it; hand-written initializers get a zero (and a `-Wmissing-field-initializers` warning where it is
  enabled).
- **The first configure with shaders on a machine builds DXC** from source (about 2 minutes on an M4 Pro, about
  365 MB of scratch space, 27 MB kept in `~/Library/Caches/Quench`, also used by later builds and other checkouts);
  `QUENCH_TOOL_CACHE`, `QUENCH_RAY_QUERY_DXC` and `QUENCH_RAY_QUERY_SHADERS` control it. Shader entries may be marked
  `<entry>:<stage>:rayquery` in `quench_add_shaders` (compiled by DXC; left out without it).
- Build: the engine enables Objective-C++ and links Metal, QuartzCore and Foundation (and MetalFX, except for the
  iOS Simulator, whose SDK lacks it) into `quench_gpu` on Apple platforms; `quench_gpu` compiles one shader set of
  its own (`gpu_blit`) with the game's shader toolchain settings.
- New, opt-in: the native Metal backend (`gpu::DeviceDesc::backend`: `gpu::Backend::Default`, `SdlGpu`, `Metal`;
  `app::AppDesc::gpuBackend`). A game that called SDL GPU directly (the rules forbid it) would not work on it.
- New in `quench::gpu`: `Caps::backend`, `gpuPassTiming`, `rayTracing`, `spatialUpscaling`, `temporalUpscaling`;
  `GpuTiming`, `Device::gpuTimings()`, `CommandBuffer::beginTiming()` / `endTiming()`; `AccelerationStructure`
  (with `AccelerationStructureDesc`, `RayGeometry`, `RayInstance`), `CommandBuffer::beginAccelerationStructurePass()`,
  `RenderPass::bindAccelerationStructure()` and `ComputePass::bindAccelerationStructure()`; `Upscaler`
  (`UpscalerDesc`, `UpscaleDesc`, `CommandBuffer::upscale()`);
  `TextureUsage::ShaderWrite`. Scenario values `perf_pass_<range>_p50`, `perf_pass_sum_p50`, `perf_pass_span_p50`,
  `perf_pass_share_p50`.
- New in `quench_render`: `RenderSettings::sunShadows`, `shadowRays`, `shadowSoftness`, `ambientOcclusion`,
  `occlusionRays`, `occlusionRadius`, `occlusionStrength`, `upscaling`; the same in `ActiveEffects` after the
  fallbacks; `RenderStats::rayInstances`, `rayBuilds`, `rayRefits`. The defaults are unchanged (shadow maps, no
  occlusion, bilinear scaling).
- A game moves its FetchContent pin to the commit of `v0.7.0`.

## Known limitations

- Ray tracing covers sun shadows and ambient occlusion only: no reflections or indirect light, and local lights
  stay unshadowed. The normal comes from the depth; only the standard surface's opacity is read by the rays (a
  custom surface that cuts holes its own way is opaque to them); at most 8 textures per frame in the ray table; no
  vertex deformation besides skinning; skinned casters only within the shadow distance. One ray per pixel without
  TAA is visibly noisy.
- No compaction of bottom levels and no refit of the top level (rebuilt every frame).
- MetalFX temporal costs about 2.1 ms at a 1080p output on the M4 Pro whatever the input size: in the demo's scene a
  quality option, not a speed-up.
- The native backend's timestamps cover the frame's main command buffer only (not the skinning pass's own command
  buffer or `Device::upload`), at most 512 samples per command buffer.
- Carried over: stage 6's shader hot reload, ASTC and Basis Universal textures, normal maps in the standard surface
  and asset hot reload on a device; stage 7's browser audio, HRTF, reverb and Vorbis; stage 8's retargeting, 2D
  blend spaces, animation LOD, dual quaternion skinning and blend shapes. The demo's macOS package is signed ad hoc
  and not notarized; its iOS package is development-signed and runs only on the devices of its profile. Windows,
  Linux, Android and browsers have not been run.
