# Quench 0.12.0

2026-11-08. Stage 14 of the [roadmap](../roadmap.md), particles and trails, in a new headless module and the
renderer, with the demo's Particles section: sparks, smoke, fireflies and twinkling fields that drift in the wind,
and glowing ribbons behind moving points that fade toward the tail and shed sparkles. With it, what the first game
on the engine (grassy isles above a sea of clouds) needed from it: a camera above the clouds that sees the sun and
the clear sky, the clouds' shadow on high ground, view rays that keep their precision far from the world's origin,
the weather's default sounds cooked from a game's own build, and trails that fade where they point along the view.
Also the rain, the clouds in motion and the shafts of rain and snow reworked after a user report on the demo's
Weather section, and ImGui ID conflicts found in scripted runs. Source only. Changes since [0.11.0](v0.11.0.md) (tag
`v0.11.0`). Everything was tested on the Mac (Apple M4 Pro) and in the iOS Simulator, the headless modules also as
WebAssembly under Node.js. The engine's tests and demo have not run on an iPhone with this release's code (see
"What was not verified").

## What changed

### Particles and trails (stage 14)

- **A new headless module, `quench_particles`, and particles and trails in the renderer**
  ([Particles](../particles.md)). `Scene::addParticles(particles::ParticleDesc, transform)` registers a system:
  spawns by rate, per metre the emitter moves, bursts and points a game gives, in a point, sphere, box, disc or
  along a path, deterministic and frame-rate independent; gravity, drag, curl noise and the weather's wind; size and
  HDR color over the life, twinkle, additive or alpha (lit) blending, camera-facing or velocity-stretched quads,
  procedural sprites, a soft fade against the scene. `Scene::addTrail(particles::TrailDesc)` and `moveTrail` draw a
  smooth camera-facing ribbon behind a point the game moves, thinning and fading toward the tail, with a glow at the
  head and sparkles shed into a system. `particles::Path` is a centripetal Catmull-Rom curve for emitters and games.
- The renderer steps systems by compute (`RenderSettings::particles = Gpu`, the default) where
  `gpu::Caps::computeShaders`, else on the CPU with the same functions (`shaders/quench/ParticleField.h`), and draws
  them after TAA and upscaling, before bloom; not in the ray tracing structures, off in the path tracer's frames.
  65536 slots per system and 262144 per scene. `RenderSettings::particleDensity`, `RenderStats::particleSystems`,
  `particles`, `particleSlots`, `particleSpawns`, `trails`, `trailVertices`; GPU ranges `particles_sim` and
  `particles`; Tracy zones `render.particles` and `render.particles.step`.
- Trails fade out where they point along the view (the sine of their angle to it from 0.3 down to 0.08): seen from
  behind a point flying away from the camera, a wide strip no longer fans out into a starburst of thin quads round
  the point or folds into wedges where it bends (found on the first game's light trail).
- **The demo's section 15, Particles** ([Engine demo](../demo.md#sections)): light streams along closed paths with
  braided heads, a sparkle field, fireflies and a campfire with sparks and smoke at the blue hour. A batch part
  `particles` with two golden captures (`demo_particles.png`, `demo_particles_fire.png`; SDL GPU's Metal driver and
  the native backend share them, Vulkan through MoltenVK has its own), a benchmark region,
  `scenarios/particles_cost.txt` (the cost on the GPU and the CPU, by density and by part, offscreen) and a check
  mode item `particles.motion` (52 items now). The menu has 15 entries (`demo_ui.png` changed in both golden sets).
  In the compact layout the menu sheet shows its buttons above the list of sections, so that a phone in landscape
  reaches them without scrolling.

### Weather and clouds

- **A camera above the clouds** ([Weather](../weather.md)). `Weather::coverAbove(point, radius)`: the cover of the
  clouds above a point (each sample's coverage times the share of its clouds' height above it), the same as `cover`
  at and under the cloud base, 0 over the tops. The renderer dims the sun and greys the sky's light by it, so a
  camera flying over a sea of clouds sees the sun and the clear sky (before, the clouds below dimmed them too). The
  sky pass draws the volumetric clouds below the horizon when the camera is above the cloud base, and the 2D layer,
  seen from above its height, is drawn below the horizon and lit on its tops. Under the cloud base nothing changes.
- **The clouds' shadow on high ground** ([Weather](../weather.md#light-under-the-clouds)).
  `RenderSettings::cloudShadowHeight` (0 m by default): the cloud shadow map lies on that plane and holds only the
  clouds above it, so ground above a sea of clouds is no longer shaded by the clouds below it. With 0 nothing
  changes.
- **The rain, the clouds in motion and the shafts** (a user report on the demo's Weather section, on the Mac: the
  rain looked unrealistic, the clouds hatched while the view moved, the shafts dotted; [Weather](../weather.md)).
  The rain's streaks are a sixtieth of a second of a drop's path, a drop's size through a lens, soft, at most 0.9
  opaque, at least 1.5 px wide, lit by the clouds' bases instead of the sky's ambient term (pi times as bright under
  overcast), denser near the camera; the splashes 6 cm for 0.12 s. The volumetric clouds' accumulation reads the
  march's noise at a new place every frame, its history through a Catmull-Rom filter and this frame through a tent:
  no hatching or smear while the view turns. The shafts of rain and snow end on the ground plane where a ray misses
  the scene, fade their noise and the clouds' shadow with the step, move their noise every frame and are smoothed at
  their reduced size: no dotted rays or dotted band at the horizon. `quenchCloudShadow` fades to the mean light over
  its map's outer twentieth instead of clamping. The shafts cost up to about 11 % more (the snow's 10 to 15 %), the
  clouds 2 to 4 %.

### Rendering

- **View rays far from the world's origin.** `quenchViewRay` (the sky, the clouds, games' shaders), the clouds' march
  and reprojection rays and the rain shafts' rays are read at depth 0 of the renderer's infinite reversed projection,
  where the inverse view-projection gives a direction without the camera's translation. Before, a point on the near
  plane minus the camera's position lost the direction's precision away from the origin (about 2 mrad at 2.2 km,
  different for every TAA jitter offset and every sub-millimetre move of the camera): over a sea of clouds the
  clouds below shook up and down, and the clouds' temporal accumulation multiplied the error. Near the origin the
  image is the same apart from the volumetric clouds, which sat about a pixel off before.

### App

- **ImGui ID conflicts in scripted runs.** Two ImGui items with the same ID in one frame (a label repeated in one
  window) were reported by ImGui only under the mouse. `quench_imgui` is built with `IMGUI_ENABLE_TEST_ENGINE` and
  the engine's hooks, which the app turns on in scripted runs and builds with assertions: each shared ID is logged
  once as an error and counted in the scenario value `imgui_id_conflicts` ([App shell](../app.md#imgui)). The demo's
  Weather panel had one (its Cirrus preset and slider) and its batches now expect none.

### Fixes

- **`quench_weather_sounds()` from a game:** the function read its script's directory from a normal variable set in
  `cmake/QuenchCook.cmake`'s scope, which a game that fetches the engine does not see, so a game's build failed on
  `'/assets/make_weather_sounds.py'`. The variable is a cache one now; a game calls the function from its own
  directories.

## What was not verified

- **Phones:** the engine's tests and demo have not run on an iPhone with this release's code. The iPhone 18 Pro Max
  has run nothing of this or the previous release; the device test app last ran on the iPhone 17 on 2026-11-07.
- **Particles and trails:** nobody has looked at them on the screen or in motion (the check mode's
  `particles.motion` asks for it); their costs are offscreen on the Mac only, none on a phone.
- **The rain, clouds and shafts after the report** were looked at in offscreen captures on the Mac only (still and
  after a few frames of motion), not on the screen, in motion or on a phone; the user has not looked at them again.
  Their costs on the Mac only.
- **The cloud shadow map has one plane:** ground far above or below `cloudShadowHeight` is still shaded by the
  column its sun ray crosses at that height. Not run on a phone.
- **The view ray fix** was measured offscreen on the Mac; nobody has looked at it on the screen yet. The engine still
  renders in world coordinates: positions near the camera keep a float's precision at their distance from the
  origin (0.24 mm at 2.2 km), so worlds of tens of kilometres would want camera-relative rendering.
- **The release package** was started once offscreen on the Mac by `tools/package_demo.sh`; the iOS package was
  built and signed, not installed on a phone.

## Known issues

- **A Vulkan validation error, once:** in one of three batch runs on 2026-11-08 (Vulkan through MoltenVK, during the
  rain part's shelter captures) the validation layer reported `VUID-vkCmdDraw-None-09600`: textures of the clouds'
  history, the rain occlusion map and the clouds' depth bound for sampling while in `TRANSFER_SRC_OPTIMAL` layout.
  The runs before and after it had none. Not explained. MoltenVK is a translation layer over Metal, not a Vulkan
  driver.
- **Particles:** not in the ray tracing structures or the path tracer; no sorting within a system (systems and trails
  are sorted by one distance each); every slot of a drawn system is stepped and drawn (no compaction or indirect
  draws); no collisions; trails are built on the CPU; the particles' clock wraps every 4096 s
  ([Particles](../particles.md#limitations)).
- **The weather after the fixes:** a snow shower's shaft seen from afar reads as a band with a flat top (the shower's
  cell under the cloud base); in a dark storm the streaks are hard to see; the shafts' rays past the demo's finite
  ground end on a plane at y = 0; the splashes are one procedural crown, repeated; the drops follow the gusts
  without inertia. The HDR pass's cost in the wind's meadow was 0.02 to 0.07 ms higher after the fixes in two runs
  (not settled).

## Changes for games

What a game on 0.11.0 changes or checks when it moves to 0.12.0. Particles are optional: a scene without particle
systems or trails renders as before. What changes without a game asking: the look of rain, snow, shafts and
volumetric clouds (a game's golden captures with them change), the precision of view rays, and errors logged for
ImGui ID conflicts in scripted runs.

- **Particles and trails (new, optional):** `quench_render` links the new `quench_particles` publicly (nothing to
  add); `quench/render/Scene.h` includes `quench/particles/Particles.h` and `Trail.h`. New headers
  `quench/particles/Particles.h` (`ParticleDesc`, curves, `kMaxSceneParticles`), `Emitter.h`, `Simulation.h`,
  `Trail.h` (`TrailDesc`, `Trail`), `Path.h` and the shader header `quench/ParticleField.h`. New in `render::Scene`:
  `addParticles`, `setParticles`, `setParticleTransform`, `setParticlesEmitting`, `setParticlesVisible`,
  `burstParticles`, `emitParticles`, `restartParticles`, `removeParticles`, `particlesAlive`, `particleDesc`,
  `addTrail`, `setTrail`, `moveTrail`, `setTrailEmitting`, `setTrailVisible`, `clearTrail`, `setTrailSparkles`,
  `removeTrail`, `trail`, `contains` for both handles, `particleSystemCount`, `particleSlots`, `trailCount`; handles
  `render::ParticleSystemId` and `TrailId`. New in `render`: `ParticleSimulation` (`Off`, `Gpu`, `Cpu`),
  `RenderSettings::particles` (`Gpu` by default) and `particleDensity`, `ActiveEffects::particles`, and
  `RenderStats::particleSystems`, `particles`, `particleSlots`, `particleSpawns`, `trails` and `trailVertices`. A
  game's own `quench::render` names that clash with these would need renaming. How to use them:
  [Particles](../particles.md#use) and the skill's reference.
- **`gpu::Caps::computeShaders`** is a new member, declared before `bcTextures`: true on SDL GPU's drivers and the
  native Metal backend. A game that builds `Caps` with positional initializers (in its own tests, say) adds it.
- **`Weather::coverAbove(point, radius)`** is new; `cover` is unchanged. The renderer dims the sun
  and the sky's light by `coverAbove` at the camera: under the cloud base the same as before, above it brighter.
- **`RenderSettings::cloudShadowHeight`** (0 by default): with the default the shadow is the same as before. A game
  whose ground lies high sets its ground's height. A game shader that reads the map through `quenchCloudShadow`
  needs no change; one that reads `FrameUniforms::cloudShadow` itself finds the map's corner followed along the sun
  from that plane down to y = 0 (the same as before with the default height). `quenchCloudShadow` fades to the mean
  light over the map's outer twentieth instead of clamping at its edge: surfaces near the edge of the map read a
  softer shadow.
- **`quenchViewRay`** returns the same directions, more precisely; a game shader that builds its own view rays from
  `FrameUniforms::invViewProj` at the near plane should read them at depth 0 or call `quenchViewRay`. A game's golden
  captures with volumetric clouds may move by about a pixel.
- **Rain, snow, shafts and volumetric clouds look different** (the streaks, splashes, the clouds' accumulation and
  the shafts above): a game's golden captures with them change, and the shafts cost up to about 11 % more (the
  snow's 10 to 15 %), the clouds 2 to 4 %. No API changed.
- **`quench_weather_sounds()`** works from a game's own CMake directories now; a game that worked around the missing
  script path can drop the workaround.
- **ImGui:** `quench_imgui` defines `IMGUI_ENABLE_TEST_ENGINE` (public) and implements ImGui's test engine hooks; a
  game that links ImGui's own test engine or defines those hooks itself would get duplicate symbols. In scripted
  runs and in builds with assertions the app logs an error for every ImGui ID two items share, once per ID; the new
  scenario value `imgui_id_conflicts` counts them (`expect imgui_id_conflicts == 0`). A game whose checks fail on
  any error line fixes its shared IDs (`##` suffixes or `PushID`).
- **The skill** (`.agents/skills/quench-game/`) describes particles, trails and `cloudShadowHeight`; a game copies it
  again when it moves its pin.
