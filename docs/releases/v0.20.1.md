# Quench 0.20.1

2026-11-14. Three defects of the demo that the user found in the published web demo with a gamepad and a mouse.
Changes since [0.20.0](v0.20.0.md). Source only. No engine module changed: the fixes are in `examples/demo`.

Released without the full gate, by the user's decision, as 0.20.0 was.

## What changed

- **The left stick moved the view the wrong way.** In every section with a free camera the stick to the right
  moved the camera's target to the left and the stick forward moved it toward the camera: the pan took the stick as
  a drag, which moves the scene with the hand. A stick now moves the view the way it is pushed. The right stick,
  which turned as a look does, is unchanged.
- **Dragging an item in the Game UI section also turned the camera.** A section can now say that the pointer is on
  its own interface (`Section::pointerOnInterface()`); the camera leaves that press or drag alone. The section's
  touch stick is off until switched on, so a mouse's drag on the scene stays the camera's.
- **The D-pad did nothing in the Game UI section**, whose text names it: the demo took every D-pad press for its
  own windows. A section can keep the D-pad (`Section::ownsDpad()`); the Game UI section does while its Focus is
  on, and it is on from the start. Tab still hands the keys and the gamepad to the demo's windows.
- The Game UI section's golden image was a picture of the second defect (the camera turned toward the horizon by
  the scenario's drags) and is replaced; the part now checks that the camera's yaw does not change.
- The demo answers the scenario values `camera_target_x` and `camera_target_z`.

## Changes for games

None.

## What was verified

From the [2026-11-14 devlog](../../devlog/2026-11-14.md), on the Mac (Apple M4 Pro), Release, by scripted input:

- The stick, measured in the shadows section (0.5 s at full deflection): before, right moved the target 5.8 m to
  the camera's left and forward 5.8 m toward the camera; after, 5.8 m to its right and 5.8 m forward.
- `scenarios/hud.txt`: 31 of 31, with the camera's yaw unchanged through its clicks and drags and the gamepad's
  focus carrying an item with the Focus on from the start.
- The three demo batches and the hud, cycle, menu and ui parts: 14 of 15 (`demo_cycle_vulkan`, below).
- The Mac package from this code: its smoke run from a fresh unzip, 27 passed, 0 failed.

## What was not verified

- **No real gamepad or mouse, and not in a browser**, where the defects were found. A gamepad's D-pad reaches a
  browser build through the browser's own mapping, which was not looked at: if it still does nothing there, that
  is a further defect.
- With a finger the camera learns of the interface a frame late and may turn by that frame's movement.
- The gate was not run (as for 0.20.0: no `tools/check.sh`, Debug, sanitizers, iOS, `step web`).
- `demo_cycle_vulkan` is unstable on Vulkan through MoltenVK: `d_memory_mb < 64 (actual 111.047)` in the round
  that resets every section, where SDL GPU's Metal driver has +6.8 MB and the native backend +2.3 MB in the same
  run, with the GPU resources and the scene's instances equal in every round. Since 0.20.0 a round resets four
  more sections. The bound is unchanged.
