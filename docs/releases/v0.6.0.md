# Quench 0.6.0

2026-11-06. Stage 8 of the [roadmap](../roadmap.md): skeletal animation, and cut-out and translucent shadows in the
standard frame. Source only. Changes since [0.5.0](v0.5.0.md) (tag `v0.5.0`). Animation is checked by numbers and
still captures: nobody has watched it in motion yet, and nothing of this release has run on an iPhone (see "What was
not verified").

## What changed

- **`quench_animation`** ([Animation](../animation.md)), headless (also in the WebAssembly build), on ozz-animation
  0.17.0. `AnimationSystem` owns the characters and updates them all in parallel on the job system (also with zero
  workers). Skeletons, clip sets and animation graphs are asset types of the game's `AssetManager` (the rest pose and
  a log line while loading and after a failure; budgets). Sampling and 1D blend trees with phase sync; state
  machines in JSON with layers, conditions, triggers, exit times and cross-fades
  ([Graphs](../animation.md#graphs-state-machines)); masked and additive layers; events in order, across loops and
  with large steps ([Events](../animation.md#events)); root motion.
- **IK and attachments** ([IK, foot placement and attachments](../animation.md#ik-foot-placement-and-attachments)).
  Two-bone and aim IK, look-at, foot placement on a ground query the game gives, and bone attachments
  (`jointWorld()`).
- **Cook.** `quench_cook skeleton` and `quench_cook animation` build skeletons and clips from glTF; clip settings
  (loop, additive, events, root motion) come from `<source>.anim.json`; the output is deterministic.
  `quench_cook model` keeps skins: `.qmodel` version 2 ([Cooking](../animation.md#cooking)).
  `quench_cook_assets(... SKELETONS ... ANIMATIONS ...)` adds them to a game's cooked assets.
- **Compute in `quench::gpu`.** Compute pipelines and passes with read-write storage buffers; `quench_shaderc` and
  `quench_add_shaders()` compile `name:compute` entries (`QUENCH_STAGE_COMPUTE`, `QUENCH_RW_STORAGE(n)`).
- **GPU skinning in `quench_render`** ([Skinned meshes](../render.md#skinned-meshes),
  [GPU skinning](../animation.md#gpu-skinning)). A skinned mesh in the retained scene gets a joint palette per
  instance (`Scene::setJointPalette()`, `SceneAssets::setJointPalette()` for cooked models); a compute pass poses
  every drawn skinned instance before the frame, and every pass (the shadow casters included) draws the posed
  vertices. Motion vectors come from the previous pose (TAA), culling bounds from the palette; `RenderStats` reports
  the skinned instances and vertices and the skinning pass's GPU time.
- **Engine demo** ([Engine demo](../demo.md)). A tenth section, "Animation" (key 0): a procedural mannequin (CC0,
  generated at build time) walks a circle by root motion with a speed blend of idle, walk and run, jumps, waves with
  the right arm on a masked layer, leans on an additive layer, looks at the camera, holds a torch, plays footstep
  sounds from events, and stands with foot placement on a ramp of 0 to 30 degrees and on a step; a crowd of up to
  512 shows the update and skinning times. Its scripted check (21 checks) runs on Metal and on Vulkan through
  MoltenVK, with a benchmark region of 256 characters. The other sections' goldens differ from 0.5.0 only in the
  menu's tenth line (and the Assets panel's sound count), except the Materials section (below).

## Cut-out and translucent shadows

A fix after a user report from the iPhone (the demo's "Materials and lights" section): the alpha-tested fence cast a
solid shadow without its holes, and the semi-transparent glass torus cast none
([Cut-out and translucent shadows](../render.md#cut-out-and-translucent-shadows)).

- Cut-out materials (`alphaCutoff > 0`) cast shadows with their holes: they go through a mask pipeline that
  evaluates the surface (`surface_shadow_fs`) and discards like the main pass.
- Alpha-blended materials cast translucent, tinted shadows: each cascade gets a transmittance map (`Rgba16Float`,
  half the shadow map's size) that the receivers read. Where the device cannot render and sample that format, the
  opacity is dithered into the depth instead (a fallback by `gpu::Caps`, shown as "(fallback)" in the demo's panel).
- `MaterialDesc::shadows` (`ShadowMode`: `Auto`, `None`, `Solid`, `Translucent`) replaces `castsShadows`; `Auto`
  takes the blend mode (opaque: solid, alpha blend: translucent, additive: none).
- Custom surface shaders get the same with two more entries (`surface_shadow_fs`, `surface_transmittance_fs`).
  Skinned casters work in every shadow path.

## What was not verified

- Nothing was watched in motion by a person: foot sliding, pops at speed changes and at the jump, the cross-fades,
  the IK on the ramp and the step, the look-at and the held torch were checked by numbers (scenario values, test
  tolerances) and in still captures only. The footstep sounds were not listened to.
- Nothing of 0.6.0 has run on an iPhone (nor 0.4.0's asset system or 0.5.0's audio): the animation tests, compute
  skinning on the A20 Pro and its GPU time, the demo's Animation section and its benchmark region, and the shadow
  fix itself (the report came from the iPhone; the fix was seen in Mac captures and run in the iOS Simulator).
- The dithered fallback is never chosen on the Mac or in the Simulator (both render and sample `Rgba16Float`
  arrays): it ran only when asked for, and through effect selection with a device that lacks the format.
- Only the procedural mannequin went through the cook: no character made in a DCC tool (non-identity rest
  rotations, scaled joints, several skins) beyond the cook tests' small files.
- The GPU cost of the shadow passes alone is not measured (the benchmark gives the frame's GPU time); the
  benchmarks ran on a busy Mac with a spread between runs larger than most differences. DXIL is built, never run.

## Changes for games

What a game on 0.5.0 changes or checks when it moves to 0.6.0:

- **`MaterialDesc::castsShadows` is removed.** `MaterialDesc::shadows` (`ShadowMode`: `Auto` by default, `None`,
  `Solid`, `Translucent`) replaces it: `castsShadows = false` becomes `shadows = ShadowMode::None`. Behaviour
  changes: alpha-blended materials now cast translucent shadows by default (before: none), and cut-out materials
  cast shadows with holes (before: solid); additive ones still cast none. `InstanceDesc::castsShadows` is unchanged.
- **Custom surface shaders.** `FrameUniforms` has two more `vec4` after `options` (`shadowTransmittance`,
  `transmittanceBias`): shaders that read it through `quench/SceneUniforms.h` only need a rebuild. The standard
  surface reads sampler 5 (the transmittance map), and `DrawUniforms::info.z` is 1 in the shadow passes
  (`surface_vs` takes the cascade's matrix then). Surfaces built with `surface_vs:vertex surface_fs:fragment` keep
  working; for cut-out or translucent materials on them, also build `surface_shadow_fs:fragment
  surface_transmittance_fs:fragment` and pass them as `SurfaceDesc::shadowFragmentShader` and
  `SurfaceDesc::transmittanceShader`, or the shadows use the standard surface's opacity.
- **`.qmodel` version 2.** The cook now writes version 2 for every model (`ModelSkin`, `ModelSkinVertex`,
  `ModelMesh::skin` and `skinning`, `ModelData::skins`), which 0.5.0 cannot read. Games cook at build time, so a
  rebuild re-cooks; version 1 files still load. `quench_cook model` refuses `--scale` for skinned models.
- **`ShaderResources` has three new members** (`readWriteStorageTextures`, `readWriteStorageBuffers`,
  `threadCount`): tables generated by `quench_add_shaders` include them; hand-written initializers get zeros (and a
  `-Wmissing-field-initializers` warning where it is enabled).
- New module `quench_animation` (headless): `target_link_libraries(game PRIVATE quench_animation)`, headers in
  `include/quench/animation/` (`AnimationSystem.h`, `Skeleton.h`, `Clip.h`, `Graph.h`, `Ik.h`). The app shell has no
  animation system: a game creates `AnimationSystem(app.assets(), app.jobs())` and updates it. It registers the asset
  types `"skeleton"` (`.qskel`), `"animation"` (`.qanim`) and `"animgraph"` (graph JSON).
- New in `quench_render`: `MeshDesc::skin` and `MeshDesc::jointCount` (after the existing fields, so positional
  initializers keep compiling), `render::SkinVertex`, `Scene::setJointPalette()`, `Scene::jointCount()`,
  `SceneAssets::setJointPalette()`, `ModelAsset::skins()`, `ModelAsset::Part::skin`;
  `RenderSettings::translucentShadows` and `ActiveEffects::translucentShadows` (`TranslucentShadows::Map`,
  `Dithered`); `RenderStats::skinnedInstances`, `skinnedVertices`, `skinningGpuMs`, `transmittanceInstances` and
  `transmittanceDraws`.
- New in `quench::gpu`: `ShaderStage::Compute`, `ComputePipeline`, `ComputePipelineDesc`,
  `Device::createComputePipeline()`, `CommandBuffer::beginComputePass()`, `ComputePass`, `StorageBufferWrite`,
  `BufferUsage::ComputeStorageRead` and `ComputeStorageWrite`. Shaders: `quench_add_shaders(... ENTRIES
  name:compute)`, `QUENCH_STAGE_COMPUTE`, `QUENCH_RW_STORAGE(n)` in `quench/Bindings.hlsli`, `quench_shaderc --stage
  compute`.
- CMake: `quench_cook_assets(... SKELETONS ... ANIMATIONS ... [SKELETON file])`, `quench_cook skeleton`,
  `quench_cook animation [--skeleton FILE] [--clips FILE]`. Every configure now also downloads ozz-animation 0.17.0
  (MIT; headless and web builds included), built as `quench_ozz` and `quench_ozz_offline`; files that include its
  headers build with `-fwrapv` (private to `quench_animation`).
- No new command line flags or scenario commands.
- A game moves its FetchContent pin to the commit of `v0.6.0`.

## Known limitations

- Animation, not done: retargeting (clips bind to the skeleton they were cooked for), 2D blend spaces, sync markers
  beyond normalized time, animation LOD (every character is sampled and skinned every frame), dual quaternion
  skinning, blend shapes, more than four joints per vertex, storage textures in compute, a CPU skinning fallback
  (every SDL GPU backend has compute).
- Animation, simplifications: foot placement casts one ray per leg (no prediction of the next footfall, no heel and
  toe); a cross-fade runs to its end before the next transition is checked; events come from states at a weight of
  0.5 or more; one skinning dispatch per instance, and the skinning output holds every drawn skinned vertex twice
  (this frame and the last). One `SceneAssets` per `AssetManager`, so a file two scenes use loads twice.
- Translucent shadows are one layer per texel, without thickness or absorption by distance; a closed glass object
  attenuates once per face toward the sun. The maps have half the shadow map's resolution (24 MiB at the default
  2048 and 3 cascades), so their edges are softer. Under TAA, the background seen through the holes of a moving
  cut-out object may smear (its motion vectors are those of the full triangles; this was so before).
- Not done from stage 6: shader hot reload, ASTC and Basis Universal textures, normal maps in the standard surface,
  asset hot reload on a device. From stage 7: browser audio, HRTF, reverb, Vorbis. Only one GPU backend (SDL GPU).
  The demo's macOS package is signed ad hoc and not notarized; its iOS package is development-signed and runs only
  on the devices of its profile. Windows, Linux, Android and browsers have not been run.
