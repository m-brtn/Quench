# Quench 0.5.0

2026-11-06. Stage 7 of the [roadmap](../roadmap.md): audio. Source only. Changes since [0.4.0](v0.4.0.md) (tag
`v0.4.0`). Every audio check so far is by measurement: nothing has been listened to, and nothing of this release has
run on an iPhone (see "What was not verified").

## What changed

- **`quench_audio`** ([Audio](../audio.md)), headless (also in the WebAssembly build). `AudioSystem`: a 32-bit float
  stereo mixer at 48 kHz with voices resampled per voice; a bus tree (`BusDesc`, `setBusVolume()`, `setBusMuted()`,
  `setBusPaused()`, level meters in `BusInfo`); one-shot and looping sounds (`play()`) with click-free volume, pitch
  and pan changes, fades and pause; streamed music with crossfades (`playMusic()`, `stopMusic()`); sources the game
  generates (`Generator`, `PcmStream`); statistics (`AudioStats`: voices, stolen and rejected sounds, underruns,
  clipped samples, mixing time).
- **3D sound** ([3D sound](../audio.md#3d-sound)). One `Listener`, `Emitter`s with four attenuation models,
  constant-power panning and Doppler (`spatialize()`), and a voice limit with priorities and stealing
  ([Voice limit and priorities](../audio.md#voice-limit-and-priorities)).
- **Sounds as assets.** `SoundAsset` is an asset type of the `AssetManager` (WAV, FLAC and MP3 decoded on jobs,
  `decodeSound()`); music streams through `quench::fs` instead of being loaded whole. `encodeWav()` writes WAV files;
  `measureLevels()` and `dominantFrequency()` analyse a mix.
- **`quench_audio_device`** ([Output](../audio.md#output)). Output through miniaudio 0.11.25 (Core Audio on Apple
  platforms; `null` is a device thread without sound) and the iOS audio session (`SessionCategory`, `Ambient` by
  default; interruptions and route changes are handled). The device's callback thread is the one exception to the
  job-system rule: it only mixes, without allocation, locks or waits ([Threads](../audio.md#threads)).
- **In the app** ([App shell](../app.md)). `App::audio()` and `App::audioDevice()`, configured by `AppDesc::audio`
  and `AppDesc::audioDevice`; the mix updates every frame after `Game::update` and stops in the background. Flags
  `--no-audio` (offline mixing on the frame clock, discarded), `--audio-device NAME` and `--audio-capture FILE.wav`
  (offline mixing on the frame clock written to a WAV file: deterministic in scripted runs). Scenario command
  `audio_measure SECONDS [MIN_HZ MAX_HZ]` and the `audio_*` values (levels per channel, peak, the strongest
  frequency, voices, underruns, clipping).
- **Files.** `quench::fs::Reader` reads a file in pieces (`fs::openAsset()`, `fs::makeMemoryReader()`); the SDL and
  standard library backends implement `Backend::open()`.
- **Cook.** `quench_cook sound` checks a WAV, FLAC or MP3 file by decoding it and keeps its bytes, or writes a
  16-bit WAV (from another format, or mixed to mono or stereo with `--channels`; the sample rate is kept);
  `quench_cook_assets(... SOUNDS ...)` adds sounds to a game's cooked assets.
- **Engine demo** ([Engine demo](../demo.md)). A ninth section, "Audio": a tone at a distance, an emitter flying
  around the listener with Doppler, music crossfading between two tracks, buses with meters, mute and pause, and a
  burst of 40 pings for voice stealing; its sounds are procedural (CC0), written at build time. Its 39 mix checks run
  in three scenarios on Metal (`demo_audio_tone`, `demo_audio_flyby`, `demo_audio_music`), the section's capture on
  Metal and Vulkan through MoltenVK, and `demo_audio_wav_match` compares the two backends' WAV byte for byte. The
  demo's checks run with `--present-mode immediate` (shorter test times; see [Platforms](../platforms.md)); the other
  sections' goldens differ from 0.4.0 only in the menu's new line (and the Assets panel's sound line).

  244 tests each, 242 passed, 2 skipped (the audio device cases: one opt-in, one for iOS only), among them the
  render cases and the demo's checks on Metal and on Vulkan through MoltenVK with the validation layers; `wasm-node`
  under Node.js 89 tests, 83 passed, 6 skipped (the cases that need threads); in the iOS Simulator (iPhone 18 Pro
  Max, iOS 27.0) `ios-sim-tests` 160 tests, 156 passed, 4 skipped (window and headroom cases without an app

  `asan`, `tsan`, `headless`, `ios-sim-release` and `ios-device-release` builds; `asan` and `tsan` ctest 244 tests
  each with the same two skipped, without sanitizer reports; `headless` 97 of 97; the capture checks 44 of 44;
  `tools/new_game.py`: the generated game builds and starts; `tools/package_demo.sh`: the demo's 23 checks passed.
- The demo's audio checks (offline mix, measured): the 1 kHz tone at 999.9 Hz; the 440 Hz fly-by at 30 m/s at
  481.4 Hz approaching and 405.3 Hz leaving (computed 481 and 405); the music crossfade from 110.0 to 146.9 Hz; a
  muted bus at rms 0.0002; 9 voices stolen by the burst of 40; 0 underruns, 0 clipped samples. The captured WAV is
  byte-identical on Metal and on Vulkan through MoltenVK (a test of the SPIR-V path, not of a real Vulkan driver),

  0.4255 ms (+1.3 %), CPU p99 0.557 and 0.548 ms (-1.6 %), GPU p50 0.5495 and 0.549 ms (6 + 6 runs). The demo's
  sections within 5 %, except the post section's CPU p50 in one full run (+16 to +18 %), which did not repeat in two
  separate interleaved comparisons (+2.9 % and +1.3 %). The new audio region: CPU p50 1.050 ms, p99 1.486 ms, GPU p50
  6.574 ms.

## What was not verified

- Nothing was listened to: no check by ear of the Mac's output, headphones or the iPhone. Core Audio output itself is
  not exercised by the automated runs (they use the null device or offline mixing); the opt-in device test
  (`QUENCH_TEST_AUDIO_DEVICE=1`) was not run.
- Nothing of 0.5.0 (nor of 0.4.0's asset system) has run on the iPhone: the audio session's activation, the
  Ring/Silent switch, interruptions (calls, Siri), route changes (headphones, Bluetooth), background and return. In
  the iOS Simulator only setting the session category ran.
- No browser audio: the mixer runs under Node.js only.

## Changes for games

What a game on 0.4.0 changes or checks when it moves to 0.5.0:

- **`quench_app` links `quench_audio` and `quench_audio_device`**, and the app creates the mixer and opens the
  default output at start: `App::audio()`, `App::audioDevice()` (null when the mix runs offline), `AppDesc::audio`,
  `AppDesc::audioDevice`. A game that plays sound through its own device keeps working, but two devices then play at
  once.
- **`--no-audio`, `--audio-device` and `--audio-capture` are engine flags now** (consumed before `createGame()`): a
  game flag of the same name no longer reaches the game. A game with its own audio flags of these names moves to the
  engine's, for example by wrapping its own mixer's render function in a `quench::audio::Generator` played on
  `App::audio()`, so that the engine's device, `--no-audio` and `--audio-capture` serve it.
- **A game that builds its own miniaudio library target drops it** and uses the engine's audio or links
  `quench_miniaudio`: linking two differently configured miniaudio builds succeeds without a warning but silently
  uses one configuration. The engine's miniaudio is built without the encoder: WAV writing through `ma_encoder_*`
  moves to `quench::audio::encodeWav()` or `--audio-capture`.
- Automated runs (tests, scripted checks, benchmarks) pass `--audio-device null` or `--no-audio` so they make no
  sound; the game template's smoke test does.
- The scenario command `audio_measure` and the `audio_*` values belong to the engine: a game command or value of the
  same name is no longer reached. New log lines: `audio: output '...' (...): mix N Hz, device N Hz, period N frames`
  at the start (or `audio: offline mixing on the frame clock ...`), and warnings when no output can be opened.
- `quench::fs`: new `Reader`, `openAsset()`, `makeMemoryReader()` and the virtual `Backend::open()`, whose default
  reads the whole file: existing custom backends compile unchanged.
- The new audio parameter structs (`PlayParams`, `MusicParams`, `BusDesc`, `DeviceDesc`) give every member a default,
  so designated initializers that skip members compile without `-Wmissing-designated-field-initializers` under
  newer clang.
- CMake: `quench_cook_assets(... SOUNDS ...)` and `quench_cook sound`. Every configure now also downloads miniaudio
  0.11.25 (headless and web builds included). Building the engine demo needs Python 3 (its sounds are generated);
  games do not.
- A game moves its FetchContent pin to the commit of `v0.5.0`.

## Known limitations

- Linear-interpolation resampling; stereo panning only (no HRTF, front and back sound alike), no occlusion, reverb
  or filters; one listener; no virtual voices (stolen and rejected sounds do not come back); no limiter on the
  master bus (samples are clamped and counted).
- WAV, FLAC and MP3 only (no Vorbis or Opus); MP3 loops keep the encoder delay and padding.
- Offline mixing (tests, `--audio-capture`, `--no-audio`) decodes streams on the main thread.
- `--present-mode immediate` was not paced to the display on this Mac on 2026-11-06, but was paced to 60 Hz on
  2026-11-05 (cause not known); the demo's checks pass either way and only take longer when it is paced.
- Not done from stage 6: shader hot reload, ASTC and Basis Universal textures, normal maps in the standard surface,
  asset hot reload on a device. No animation (stage 8). Only one GPU backend (SDL GPU). The demo's macOS package is
  signed ad hoc and not notarized; its iOS package is development-signed and runs only on the devices of its profile.
  Windows, Linux, Android and browsers have not been run.
