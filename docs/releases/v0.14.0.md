# Quench 0.14.0

2026-11-10. The weather's look and sound after the user's reports of the same day: the clouds are drawn in front of
mountains and under a camera that flies above them, nothing falls above the clouds, the clouds have bumps and shadow
sides and no level lines, the sky under a closed deck is as bright as the light it lets through, the rain's rings are
short wave packets and its default sound is noise without tones. Changes since [0.13.2](v0.13.2.md). Source only.

**Released without the gates** (the user's decision, 2026-11-10): no CTest run, only the checks listed below, and
the golden captures of the demo's Weather section are stale on every backend. See "What was not verified".

## What changed

- **Clouds in front of the scene.** The clouds were a part of the sky, behind all geometry. The march now also
  says where its clouds are along each ray (their mean distance and its deviation, accumulated over frames with the
  image), and in a frame whose camera or highest opaque surface in view is at or above the cloud base the HDR pass
  is cut in two: the opaque surfaces and the sky, a fullscreen pass that blends the clouds over each surface by the
  share of its ray's clouds in front of it, then the transparent surfaces and the Transparent-stage game passes.
  The 2D layer is drawn over surfaces beyond it the same way, also from above. `RenderStats::cloudsOverScene` says
  when. Other frames are drawn as before. [Weather](../weather.md#clouds-in-front-of-the-scene).
- **Cloud shadows by height.** The cloud shadow map also holds the height of each column's clouds: a surface above
  them is in the sun, one among them is shaded by the part over it. A summit over a closed deck is sunlit without
  `cloudShadowHeight`.
- **No rain or snow above the clouds.** At or above the cloud base (as in `Weather::precipitation(point)`) there
  are no streaks, splashes or flakes and surfaces neither get wet nor whiten, fading in over the 100 m below it
  (`quenchFallReach` in `quench/Rain.hlsli`; `FrameUniforms::rainGrid.w` is the cloud base). The shafts of rain and
  snow are dimmed by the clouds a camera above the base looks down on.
- **The clouds' shapes and light.** The detail noise moves a cloud's surface in and out (round bumps on cumulus
  tops, wisps at the base); clouds have a grey shadow side; the level lines over steep sides and the ripples over
  bases at a low angle are gone (the light's jitter was tied to the march's); long light steps no longer leave dark
  grain on sunlit sides at a low sun. The cirrus is drawn per pixel in the sky pass.
- **The light under the clouds in the scene's units.** A closed deck was a third as bright as the snow it lit, and
  brown at a low sun (the clouds' ambient light and `FrameUniforms::overcast` were divided by pi). The diffuse
  light in a cloud and the ground's light under a cover now follow one law by the clouds' thickness (0.31 of the
  light above under a 500 m stratus deck, 0.10 under a storm); snow on the ground sends 0.8 of its light back up.
- **The rain's rings.** A drop leaves two or three crests about 2 cm apart that run out at 0.3 m/s and are gone
  after 0.55 s, 10 to 25 cm from the drop, instead of a target of wide rings; beyond 5 to 14 m (at 1080 lines) the
  water is a duller mirror instead.
- **The rain's default sound.** The four rain loops are noise only (they were sine bursts at random pitches, a
  tune under a roof); under a full cover the open-air layers stay at 0.35 instead of silence.
- **The demo's Weather section:** a 3.4 km mountain 13 km from the place and the views "Toward the mountain" and
  "Over the clouds"; the Rain here and Passing shower presets give the sun back to the next preset.

  tests under Node.js, no iOS Simulator build or tests, no packaged demo, no new game.

  rings, the mountain): their checks fail until the captures are renewed (planned next). The render case
  `renderer_rain_wets_the_open_ground` no longer tests the rings themselves at its size.
- **Nothing was seen in motion or on a screen**, and nobody has heard the new rain sound: the clouds' edge on a
  ridge while the camera moves, the moment the cut of the pass starts when the camera climbs through the cloud
  base, the detail's bumps as the weather evolves, the rings running and fading.
- Clouds in front of the scene: one mean distance and deviation per ray (a thin cloud before a ridge that shares
  its ray with a thick one behind it is drawn thinner than it is); at the march's size; transparent surfaces are
  drawn over the clouds; the traced effects, the sky cube and the path tracer do not know of it.

## Changes for games

Nothing has to change. Behaviour that differs:

- A game's Transparent-stage pass runs in a render pass of its own (after the clouds, with the frame's bindings
  bound again) in frames with `RenderStats::cloudsOverScene`; pipelines and bindings are as before.
- A world at or above the cloud base gets no rain or snow from the renderer (as `Weather::precipitation(point)`
  already said); `cloudShadowHeight` is no longer needed to keep a surface above the clouds in the sun.
- A closed deck and the ambient light under it are brighter than before (the deck's base was a third as bright as
  the snow it lit), and the default rain sounds differ; a game with its own rain sounds (`WeatherAudio`) keeps
  them, at 0.35 of the open-air layers under a full cover.
- New: `RenderStats::cloudsOverScene`, `quenchFallReach` (`quench/Rain.hlsli`).
