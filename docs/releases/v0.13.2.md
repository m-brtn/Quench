# Quench 0.13.2

2026-11-10. Two fixes of the renderer, found in the Outpost micro game (`games/outpost`): the screen-space ambient
occlusion no longer crawls on evenly lit surfaces, and an instance or a placed model can stop casting shadows.
Changes since [0.13.1](v0.13.1.md). Source only.

**Released without the full gates** (the user's decision, 2026-11-10): only the render and demo tests of the Mac's
three backends ran. See "What was not verified".

## What changed

- **Screen-space ambient occlusion accumulates over frames.** With `AmbientOcclusion::ScreenSpace`, evenly lit
  surfaces showed blotches about 8 pixels wide that moved every frame: one frame's GTAO term (2 slices of 4 steps
  to either side over a screen radius of up to 20% of the height) is noisy, the 4 x 4 blur left blotches, and TAA
  did not average them out. More slices did not cure it and a fixed noise left the blotches standing. The term is
  now blended into a history of up to 16 frames at half resolution (`ss_gtao_temporal_fs`): each texel finds its
  surface in the history where the camera's motion puts it, checks its depth, takes in the mean of the 3 x 3 new
  terms on its surface and stays within their range. The noise changes every frame, with or without TAA. The
  blur's weights turn from equal to a tent as the history grows, which removes 2 x 2 pixel steps that the noise
  had hidden. `View::cut` starts the history over.
  [Render](../render.md#screen-space-ambient-occlusion).
- **Instances that cast no shadows can be set later, and placed models too.** `Scene::setCastsShadows(instance,
  bool)` and `SceneAssets::setCastsShadows(modelInstance, bool)` (kept when the model's parts are rebuilt). Such an
  instance is in no shadow map, not among the geometry of traced shadows and occlusion and no rain occluder.
  The need: an outline drawn as an inverted hull a few centimeters around a model shadowed the model wherever the
  shadow maps were finer than the gap (the camera close up), in bands along the facets or all over. `SceneAssets`
  had no way to place a model that casts none.

## What was verified

From the [2026-11-10 devlog](../../devlog/2026-11-10.md), on the Mac (Apple M4 Pro), offscreen:

- **The ripple, by numbers** (the Outpost game, native Metal backend, 4536 x 2835, a flat surface in ambient
  light, RMS in 8-bit levels): the image's deviation from its 11 x 11 mean 1.45 before and 0.57 after; the
  difference of two captures 7 frames apart 2.37 before and 0.48 after; without occlusion 0.42 and 0.15. Magnified
  crops before and after, a capture during a camera turn and one of a walking character were looked at.
- **Tests** (`ctest --preset release -L "render|demo" -j8`: SDL GPU's Metal driver, the native Metal backend and
  Vulkan through MoltenVK, a translation layer over Metal): 403 of 403, the skipped ray tracing cases of the
  backends that cannot trace among them. New: `renderer_screen_space_occlusion_accumulates` (a still camera's
  frames 1 and 2 differ by 0.352 levels, frames 17 and 18 by 0.039; after a 1 m camera move the ground is occluded
  next to the box, 0.926, and not where the box was on the screen, 1.000; a cut gives a first frame's image) and
  `renderer_instance_without_shadows` (a box inside a shell 5 cm larger gets 0.000 of open ground's sunlight while
  the shell casts and 1.000 when it does not, with shadow maps and, on the native backend, with traced shadows).
- **Golden images:** `demo_raytracing_screen.png` (the screen-space fallbacks) renewed for Metal and Vulkan
  through MoltenVK: mean difference 0.446, 283 pixels over 16 levels (0.055%), within the thresholds; both images
  looked at. No other golden image moved beyond its thresholds.
- **Cost** (`examples/demo/scenarios/gtao_phone.txt`, native Metal backend, 1920 x 1080, two runs before and after
  in one session), `gtao` GPU p50: 0.413, 0.414 to 0.497, 0.496 ms in the shadows section; 0.175, 0.175 to 0.220,
  0.220 in the ray tracing section; 0.255, 0.250 to 0.300, 0.299 in the tunnel. Two more half-size `Rgba16Float`
  targets (8.3 MB at 1080p).
- **In headless Chrome on the Mac:** the Outpost game's web build with this engine passed its smoke scenario (16
  passed, 0 failed) and 6 s on the path a person's page takes, no WebGPU error. The demo's page: see "Deployed".
- `tools/format.sh --check` and `tools/check_skill.py` clean.

## What was not verified

- **The full gates did not run:** no Debug, AddressSanitizer or ThreadSanitizer build, no `wasm-node` tests under
  Node.js, no iOS Simulator build or tests, no `ios-xcode` project, no new game, no packaged demo, and not
  `tools/check.sh step web` (the demo's whole batch in headless Chrome). The release build's other labels (core,
  physics, assets, audio, animation, navigation, weather, particles, cook, gpu, app, hello) were not run either;
  none of their code changed.
- Nobody has looked at the accumulated occlusion in motion on a screen. Its history follows the camera only:
  around a moving object the occlusion settles over a few frames. The first frames after a cut show one frame's
  noise, as before.
- Not on a phone, not on Windows; SDL GPU's frame times with GTAO were not measured again.
- `SceneAssets::setCastsShadows` has no test of its own (`Scene::setCastsShadows` has; the Outpost game uses the
  former).

## Deployed

The web demo at <https://m-brtn.github.io/Quench/> is published from the tagged commit after the tag
(`tools/publish_web.sh`, whose own check runs the demo's smoke scenario in headless Chrome on the Mac); the check
of the published page is in the devlog.

## Changes for games

Nothing to change. New: `Scene::setCastsShadows` and `SceneAssets::setCastsShadows`. A game that draws shells around
its models (outlines, glows) should take them out of the shadows with them. Games that use
`AmbientOcclusion::ScreenSpace` (or the screen-space fallbacks) get the accumulated occlusion: about 0.05 to
0.08 ms more GPU time at 1080p on an M4 Pro and a few frames of settling after `View::cut` or around moving
objects.
