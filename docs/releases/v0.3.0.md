# Quench 0.3.0

2026-11-05. Stage 5 of the [roadmap](../roadmap.md): the retained scene and the standard frame (`quench_render`), HDR
display output and the engine demo. Source only. Changes since [0.2.0](v0.2.0.md) (tag `v0.2.0`).

## What changed

- **`quench_render`** ([Render module](../render.md)). A retained scene (`render::Scene`): meshes, RGBA8 textures (sRGB
  or linear, mipmaps made on the GPU), materials, instances, lights and the environment, registered by
  generation-checked handles. The standard frame (`render::Renderer`): sun shadow cascades (standard depth), the HDR
  pass with reversed depth, sky and atmosphere with a daylight palette for any sun elevation (a gradient sky as the
  fallback), height fog, motion vectors with TAA or FXAA, bloom, ACES or AgX tone mapping. Effects fall back by device
  capability (`gpu::Caps`), never by platform. Games add passes at five points of the frame (`PassStage`: shadow,
  opaque, transparent, post-HDR, overlay) and their own surface shaders, lit by the engine. CPU helpers for tests and
  tools: `MeshBuilder`, cascade splits, projections, the daylight palette, the tone curves (`toneMapSdr()`,
  `toneMapHdr()`).
- **Shader library.** `shaders/quench/` (`#include "quench/..."`): hashes, noise, BRDF terms, shadows, sky, fog, tone
  curves, the fullscreen triangle and the surface shader entry points; `SceneUniforms.h` holds uniform structs shared
  by C++ and HLSL.
- **HDR display output** ([App shell](../app.md#hdr-output)). `gpu::SwapchainComposition` (`Sdr`, `SdrLinear`,
  `HdrExtendedLinear`, `Hdr10`) and `gpu::DisplayOutput`, with `Device::supportsComposition()`, `setComposition()`
  and `composition()`. In the app: `HdrMode` (`AppDesc::hdr`, `--hdr off|auto|on`, `--hdr-headroom N`,
  `App::setHdrMode()`, `hdrMode()`, `output()`, `hdrSupported()`, `Frame::output`). In HDR the renderer's tone curve
  reaches up to the display's headroom, ImGui is composited at paper white (`ImGuiRenderer::draw()` takes the
  target's white value, default 1), and captures stay SDR (divided by paper white and clipped; the peak is logged).
  `App::displayHeadroom()` is the display's current headroom, read every frame from the screen on Apple platforms;
  `App::displayPotentialHeadroom()` is the most it can show, and `HdrMode::Auto` turns HDR on when that is above 1.
  The default output stays SDR.
- **Sun disc.** The disc is added to the sky with at least 4 times the sky's luminance at the sun; before, it
  replaced the sky, so a low sun came out darker than its glow. The vignette now darkens the scene before the tone
  curve for both curves. Test `render.renderer_sun_disc_outshines_its_glow`.
- **Scenarios** ([App shell](../app.md#scenarios)). The points of `mouse`, `click` and `touch` are window points, percent
  of the window (`X% Y%`, also for touch deltas) or names (`@NAME`, from the new optional `Game::scenarioPoint()`),
  so scripts work at any window size. Scripted touches also send the mouse events SDL makes from a real first finger,
  so ImGui sees them. New command `hdr off|on|auto`; new values `hdr`, `hdr_supported`, `hdr_headroom`,
  `display_headroom`, `display_headroom_potential`.
- **Log lines.** `app: display output: <composition> (<mode>), headroom X, paper white Y, swapchain F; display
  headroom X (potential Y), HDR [not ]supported` at the start and when the output changes; `app: display headroom X
  (potential Y); output headroom Z` when only the headroom changes (at most once a second); in HDR the `captured`
  line adds the capture's peak.
- **Engine demo** ([Engine demo](../demo.md)). `examples/demo` ("Quench Demo"): a menu of seven sections (materials and
  lights, shadows, sky and fog, post-processing, HDR output, physics, input) with ImGui controls; every section has a
  scripted check and golden captures (Metal, and Vulkan through MoltenVK); smoke and benchmark scenarios; budgets in
  [Performance](../performance.md). `tools/package_demo.sh` builds it into `dist/`: a macOS zip, and with `--ios
  --team ID` a development-signed `.ipa` (it refuses a free Personal Team and installs nothing).
- **Roadmap.** Audio is stage 7 (P0), animation 8, the native Metal backend and ray tracing 9 (with where ray tracing
  runs), the engine demo 10: started in stage 5, every stage adds its section and ends with a demo build in `dist/`.

## Changes for games

Nothing is renamed or removed. What a game on 0.2.0 checks when it moves to 0.3.0:

- `--hdr` and `--hdr-headroom` are engine flags now (consumed before `createGame()`): a game flag of the same name is
  renamed.
- The scenario command `hdr` and the values `hdr`, `hdr_supported`, `hdr_headroom`, `display_headroom` and
  `display_headroom_potential` belong to the engine: a game command or value of the same name is no longer reached.
- A scripted touch now also sends mouse events (`SDL_TOUCH_MOUSEID`), as a real first finger does: a game that
  handles mouse and touch input separately sees a scripted touch as both, as it does with a real finger.
- The log has the new `app: display output: ...` line at the start and `app: display headroom ...` lines while the
  headroom changes; tools that read the log skip them.
- The output stays SDR unless `AppDesc::hdr` or `--hdr` asks for HDR. In HDR the game's frame target holds linear
  values with paper white at `Frame::output.paperWhite`, up to `Frame::output.headroom` (`Rgba16Float`); a game that
  draws its own passes writes such values itself, `quench_render` does it for its frame.
- `ImGuiRenderer::draw()` has a new parameter with a default: existing calls compile unchanged.
- New and optional: `quench_render` (link it; game shaders include `quench/...` through `quench_add_shaders()`),
  `Game::scenarioPoint()`, `App::displayHeadroom()`, `displayPotentialHeadroom()`, `setHdrMode()`, `hdrMode()`,
  `output()`, `hdrSupported()`, the `gpu` composition API.
- A game moves its FetchContent pin to the commit of `v0.3.0`.

## Known limitations

- HDR output was not seen on a Mac display (this Mac's has no HDR range); on the iPhone the user looked at it.
  `Hdr10` output is not PQ-encoded: it gets SDR values and a warning. No auto exposure: at a low sun the disc and the
  glow next to it both clip to white in SDR (in HDR the disc stays above the glow).
- The scripted gamepad does not attach in the iOS Simulator, so scenario lines that use it fail there (they pass on
  the Mac and on the iPhone).
- In portrait on a phone the demo's windows cover much of the scene.
- Not in the render module yet: screen-space ambient occlusion, bounce light, reflections and sun shafts; cut-out
  shadows of alpha-tested materials; normal maps; motion vectors for vertex-deformed surfaces; quality presets; GPU
  timing per pass. Textures come from memory as RGBA8 (loading files is stage 6).
- No asset pipeline, job system, audio or animation (stages 6 to 8). Only one GPU backend (SDL GPU); GPU timing on
  Metal only, through SDL 3.4.18's internal fence.
- The demo's macOS package is signed ad hoc and not notarized; its iOS package is development-signed and runs only on
  the devices of its profile.
- Windows, Linux, Android and browsers have not been run.
