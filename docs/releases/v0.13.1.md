# Quench 0.13.1

2026-11-10. A fix for the web build in Safari: the page sometimes stayed black until the window was resized.
Changes since [0.13.0](v0.13.0.md). Source only. Only the WebGPU backend (web builds) changed; nothing else behaves
differently.

## What changed

- **Safari's black page.** In Safari 27.0.1 on a Mac, about 1 in 3 to 5 loads of the 0.13.0 web demo stayed black
  until the window's size changed; Chrome never did. The frames were rendered: a script in the page recorded the
  same calls in black and shown pages (one configuration of the canvas, every `getCurrentTexture` in an animation
  frame, about 59 frames per second, no WebGPU error), and a copy of the black page's canvas through `drawImage`
  was as bright as a shown one's (mean 140.2 to 140.7 of 255). Safari did not put them on the screen until a new
  size made the backend configure the canvas again. The cause inside Safari was not found. The WebGPU backend now
  configures the canvas once more, one pixel narrower and back within one frame, 20 frames after its first
  configuration (`WebGpuDevice::acquireSwapchainTexture`). The page does not show the narrower size: no frame is
  drawn at it.

## What was verified

From the [2026-11-10 devlog](../../devlog/2026-11-10.md), on the Mac (Apple M4 Pro):

- **In Safari 27.0.1, by the user's eye:** without the change 1 of 4 new tabs black and 1 to 2 of 5 reloads of
  the published page; with the same change made by a script in the page 0 of 4 new tabs; with the backend's change
  0 of 5 new tabs and none in two rounds of about ten reloads. A small sample against a failure rate of roughly 20
  to 40%, not a proof. Playwright's headless WebKit 27.2 never showed the black page (its screenshots do not go
  through Safari's compositing), so it does not check this fix.
- **In headless Chrome on the Mac** (`tools/check.sh step web`): `hello`'s check scenario 21 passed, 0 failed; the
  demo's batch 757 passed, 0 failed, no WebGPU error; 41 captures within the thresholds of the Mac's Metal golden
  images; both pages for a few seconds on the path a person's page takes.
- **The release gate** (`tools/check.sh release`'s steps, 711.7 s; its format step over the tracked files only,
  since an untracked work directory in the checkout is not formatted): debug, asan and tsan each 602 passed and
  70 skipped of 672; release 743 passed and 69 skipped of 812 (the captures of the Mac's three backends); the
  headless tests as WebAssembly under Node.js 196 passed and 9 skipped of 205; iOS Simulator 438 passed and 70
  skipped of 508; the `ios-xcode` project generated; a new game built and started; the packaged demo's smoke run
  from a fresh unzip 22 passed, 0 failed, its capture looked at.

## What was not verified

- Safari on other systems, on an iPhone or an iPad; Firefox. Whether Safari can still show a black page after
  the change, at a rate below what the sample above can see.

## Deployed

The web demo at <https://m-brtn.github.io/Quench/> is published from the tagged commit after the tag
(`tools/publish_web.sh`); the check of the published page is in the devlog.

## Changes for games

None: no public API, asset format, settings or dependency changes. A game's web build gets the fix with the
engine pin moved to 0.13.1.
