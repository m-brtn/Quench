# Quench Demo privacy policy

Quench Demo (listed as Quench — 3D Engine on the App Store) is an interactive showcase of the open-source Quench engine.
It works offline and does not require an account.

## Data collection

The app does not collect or transmit personal information. It contains no advertising, analytics service, tracking,
or in-app purchases. It does not access your contacts, location, camera, photo library, or microphone.

## Data stored on your device

The app stores your settings locally. If you use its check or benchmark modes, it also stores the results locally,
including rendering settings, device and graphics capabilities, and performance measurements. These files are not
automatically sent to us or to another service. Removing the app removes its local app data.

The engine and its platform libraries read file metadata within the app's own storage and use system timers to
animate scenes and measure performance. They do not use this information to track or identify you.

## Apple services and information you choose to share

Apple handles App Store downloads and any diagnostics you elect to share with Apple under Apple's privacy policy.
If you choose to submit a report on GitHub, the information you include is handled by GitHub and may be public.
Please avoid including private information in public reports.

## Contact

For questions about this policy, use the [Quench project issue tracker](https://github.com/m-brtn/Quench/issues).
